Cascadia Health Plan is a fictitious regional payer with 450,000 members. I assume about 5% of its professional claims, roughly 300,000 a year, still arrive as paper, fax or PDF CMS-1500 forms; the national average is about 2% (CAQH 2024), and I show that case too. The user is the claims intake processor. One product surface: the OpenAI Platform, specifically the Agents SDK on the Responses API. AWS hosts the app and the FHIR data; every model call goes to OpenAI. All data in this demo is synthetic.
